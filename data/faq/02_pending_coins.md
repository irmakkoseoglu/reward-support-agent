# Why are my coins pending?

Every offer has a hold period before its coins become available. The hold period is shown on
the offer page and is usually between 3 and 30 days. It exists because advertisers confirm
that the install or purchase was genuine before they pay us.

When the hold period ends, the coins move to your available balance automatically. You do not
need to contact support. If the advertiser rejects the completion (for example because the app
was installed before, or a purchase was refunded), the pending coins are removed and the offer
status changes to "rejected".
