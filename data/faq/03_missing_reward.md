# I completed an offer but did not get my reward

Tracking can take up to 24 hours after you complete an offer step. Please wait 24 hours first.

Common reasons a reward is not tracked:
- The app was already installed on your device before (offers are for new users only).
- You used an ad blocker, VPN or private browsing while starting the offer.
- You did not open the app at least once within 24 hours after installing it.
- You did not complete the exact requirement (for example reached level 9 instead of level 10).

If more than 72 hours have passed and the offer still shows "started", you can open a
missing-reward ticket. Include a screenshot that shows the requirement completed and the date.
Missing-reward tickets are answered within 5 business days. We cannot guarantee that a claim
is approved, because the final decision is made by the advertiser.
