# Cashout methods and minimums

| Method      | Minimum  | Processing time        | Fee  |
|-------------|----------|------------------------|------|
| PayPal      | 5.00 EUR | up to 48 hours         | none |
| Crypto (BTC, LTC) | 2.00 EUR | usually within 1 hour | network fee |
| Gift cards  | 5.00 EUR | instant                | none |

Only available coins can be cashed out, pending coins cannot.
