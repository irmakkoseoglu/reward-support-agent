# How coins work

CoinQuest rewards you with coins for trying apps, games and surveys from our partners.
1,000 coins are worth 1.00 EUR. Your balance has two parts:

- **Available coins** can be cashed out right away.
- **Pending coins** are rewards that were tracked but are still in their hold period
  (see "Why are my coins pending?").

Coins do not expire as long as you log in at least once every 12 months.
