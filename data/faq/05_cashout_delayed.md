# My cashout has not arrived

Check the status of your cashout in "Wallet > History":

- **processing**: the payout is on its way. PayPal payouts can take up to 48 hours.
- **under_review**: your first cashout, or an unusually large one, is checked manually by our
  safety team. Reviews take up to 5 business days. You will get an email when it is done.
- **completed**: we have sent the money. Check the email address of your PayPal account or
  your crypto wallet address. Completed payouts cannot be changed.
- **failed**: the payout could not be delivered, usually because of a wrong PayPal email or
  wallet address. The coins are returned to your balance and you can cash out again.

If a PayPal payout shows "processing" for more than 48 hours, open a cashout ticket.
